Who ESEA is for

ESEA is a free early-learning app for children aged 3 to 6, made to be used with a grown-up nearby. Children learn early reading, numbers, shapes, colors, feelings and the world around them through short, spoken, picture-based activities. Your child does not need to read: every question is read aloud.

Start with Today’s learning on the home screen. It picks three short activities each day (about 10 minutes), so you never have to choose.

How ESEA teaches, and why

Reading path
  1. Rhyming words
  2. Putting word parts together (sun + flower)
  3. First sounds in words
  4. Letter sounds
  5. Letters
Math path
  1. Seeing 1–5 at a glance
  2. Counting objects to 5
  3. Counting to 10
  4. Comparing: which has more?
  5. Number order: what comes next?
  6. Recognizing numbers
  • Hearing sounds before reading letters. Children who can hear rhymes and sounds in words find learning letters easier, so ESEA practises sounds first.
  • Number sense before number names. Seeing small amounts at a glance, counting real objects and comparing come before recognizing written numbers.
  • Feelings in four steps: recognize a feeling, understand what causes it, name it, and learn what helps, including calm-down tools.
  • Practice that adapts. ESEA remembers each letter, number and shape your child plays and brings back the ones they find hard. A mistake gets a hint, then the answer, never a “you lose”.
  • No points, rankings or timers. Children are praised for trying, and sessions end with “All done”.
  • Learning off the screen. Each activity comes with a short idea to try at home together.

These choices follow widely used early-childhood approaches, including research on early literacy summarized by the U.S. National Early Literacy Panel (2008), learning trajectories for early mathematics (Clements & Sarama), and the social-emotional learning framework of CASEL. ESEA is a practice tool for home, not a replacement for play, books, or school.

Privacy, in plain words

  • Nothing is sent to us. ESEA has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. It does not load any third-party scripts.
  • What is saved, and where. Your child’s first name (or any nickname), their chosen animal, their age, their progress and your settings are saved only in this browser, on this device.
  • Deleting it. In the Grown-ups area you can reset a child’s progress or remove a child. Clearing this browser’s data deletes everything.
  • The voice. ESEA speaks using voices built into your device. Some voices marked “needs internet” in the Grown-ups area (for example, some voices in Chrome and Edge) are run by the browser maker, so the words being spoken, which can include the first name you entered, go to their speech service. To keep everything on the device, choose a voice without “needs internet”.
  • Links that leave the app (a book shop and a donation page) are behind a grown-ups check. There is no chat, no messaging and nothing children can post.

Healthy screen time

The World Health Organization advises that children aged 2–4 have no more than one hour of sitting screen time a day, and less is better. ESEA is built for short sessions: lessons end after a few questions, Today’s learning takes about 10 minutes, and you can set a daily time limit in the Grown-ups area. When time is up, your child sees a calm “time for a break” screen. Sitting with your child and talking about what they see makes screen time far more valuable.

Accessibility

  • Every question and answer can be heard, and there is a button to hear it again.
  • Videos have captions of everything that is said.
  • Large buttons, readable colors, and labels for screen readers.
  • Animations are reduced when the device is set to reduce motion.
  • The voice and its speed can be changed in the Grown-ups area.

Languages

The home screen, Today’s learning and the setup are available in English, Spanish and French. The math lessons, including their praise and hints, also speak Spanish and French. Reading lessons teach English letters and sounds, and are marked EN when another language is chosen. Choose the language in setup or in the Grown-ups area.
