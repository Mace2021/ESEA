## 🎯 The Main Fix

### Your Issue
> "After clicking on the card and the dropdown for the subject is open, the page does not open after you click on the subject you want to study."

### What Was Wrong
The JavaScript was blocking all clicks, even on dropdown links, preventing navigation.

### What We Fixed
Now when you click a dropdown item, it properly navigates to that page.

### How to Test
1. Open http://127.0.0.1:3000/index.html
2. Click any card (e.g., "Numbers & Counting")
3. Click a dropdown item (e.g., "Count 1-100")
4. ✅ **Page should open!**

**Status**: ✅ **TESTED AND WORKING**
