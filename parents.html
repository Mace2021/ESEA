What to do next

Progress

Each item a child plays is marked knows after a few right first tries, learning while it settles, and practising right after a miss. Lessons automatically show practising items more often.

Children

Each child gets their own progress. Tap the animal to change it. Children choose who is playing from the home screen.

Daily time limit

When time is up, your child sees a calm "Time for a break" screen. You can add 15 more minutes from that screen with the grown-ups check. The limit is per child, per day.

Try this at home

Children learn the most when a grown-up joins in. These take a few minutes and no screen.

Privacy

ESEA has no ads, no accounts and no tracking. Names and progress are saved only in this browser on this device. They are not sent anywhere, and clearing the browser's data removes them.
