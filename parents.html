This area is for grown-ups.

What to do next

Learning paths

Two step-by-step paths, in the order young children usually learn. Each step is numbered on the home screen. Move on when a step is going well; it is fine to go back.

Progress

Each item a child plays is marked knows after a few right first tries, learning while it settles, and practising right after a miss. Lessons automatically show practising items more often.

Children

Each child gets their own progress. Tap the animal to change it. Children choose who is playing from the home screen.

Daily time limit

When time is up, your child sees a calm "Time for a break" screen. You can add 15 more minutes from that screen with the grown-ups check. The limit is per child, per day.

Practice language

For bilingual families: the math lessons (marked ES FR below) speak and show their words in the language you choose. Reading lessons stay in English, because rhymes and letter sounds are different in every language. The voice depends on the voices installed on this device.

Voice

ESEA speaks with a voice built into this device. It picks the most natural-sounding one it can find, but you can choose another and set how fast it talks. Voices named “Natural”, “Enhanced” or “Premium” usually sound the most human. More voices can often be added in the device's settings (for example on iPhone: Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices).

Use ESEA offline

After ESEA has been opened once with internet, it keeps working without internet, for example in the car or on a plane. Add it to your home screen so it opens like an app.

iPhone / iPad: in Safari, tap Share, then "Add to Home Screen". Android: in Chrome, tap the ⋮ menu, then "Add to Home screen" or "Install app". Computer: in Chrome or Edge, use the install icon in the address bar.

Try this at home

Children learn the most when a grown-up joins in. These take a few minutes and no screen.

Privacy

ESEA has no ads, no accounts and no tracking. Names and progress are saved only in this browser on this device. They are not sent anywhere, and clearing the browser's data removes them.
